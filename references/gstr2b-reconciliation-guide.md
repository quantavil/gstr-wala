# GSTR-2B vs Books Reconciliation Guide

This reference guides the reconciliation between the taxpayer's **Purchase Register (Books of Account)** and the official **GSTR-2B Statement (Auto-drafted ITC statement)** generated by the GST Portal.

---

## 1. What is GSTR-2B?

- GSTR-2B is a **static, auto-drafted ITC statement** generated on the **14th of every month** based on the GSTR-1, IFF, and GSTR-5 filings of suppliers up to the 13th of the month.
- Unlike GSTR-2A (which was dynamic and changed whenever a supplier filed late), GSTR-2B is frozen for each tax period, making it the legal benchmark for ITC claims in GSTR-3B.

---

## 2. Categorization Taxonomy & Action Rules

| Category | Definition | Action in GSTR-3B | Action in Accounting Books |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EXACT_MATCH** | GSTIN, Invoice No, Date, Taxable Value, and Tax amounts match 100%. | Claim in Table 4(A)(5). | Mark as Reconciled. |
| **TOLERANCE_MATCH** | Tax difference is $\le \pm ₹1.00$ due to line-item vs invoice-level rounding. | Claim in Table 4(A)(5) aligned to 2B value. | Auto-adjust rounding difference to Tax Rounding Off A/c. |
| **VALUE_MISMATCH** | Invoice exists in both, but Tax/Taxable difference $> ₹1.00$. | Restrict claim to lower of Books or 2B in Table 4(A)(5). | Raise vendor dispute / request debit or credit note. |
| **IN_BOOKS_ONLY** | Invoice is recorded in Books, but missing in GSTR-2B (Supplier did not file GSTR-1). | **DO NOT CLAIM** in current month (Rule 36(4)). | Transfer to "Deferred ITC / ITC Pending 2B" ledger; follow up with vendor. |
| **IN_2B_ONLY** | Invoice appears in GSTR-2B, but is not recorded in Purchase Register. | Do not claim until verified. | Check if an unrecorded purchase, expense, or incorrect GSTIN entered by supplier. |
| **BLOCKED_17_5** | Inward supply falls under Section 17(5) (motor vehicle, food catering, personal use). | Include in 4(A)(5) and permanently reverse in **Table 4(B)(1)**. | Expense out tax or capitalize to asset cost. |
| **RULE_37_REVERSAL** | Invoice unpaid to supplier for $> 180\text{ days}$. | Include in 4(A)(5) and temporarily reverse in **Table 4(B)(2)**. | Track vendor aging; re-claim in Table 4(D)(1) upon payment. |
| **INELIGIBLE_IN_2B** | 2B has `itcavl = "N"` (Section 16(4) time-bar or POS rule restriction). | Report in **Table 4(D)(2)** (Ineligible ITC). | Book tax as irrecoverable expense. |

## 3. Invoice Number Normalization Best Practices

Suppliers and buyers often format the same invoice number differently in their respective ERPs:
- Buyer records: `INV/2026/0042`
- Supplier files: `INV-2026-42` or `000042` or `42`

`gstr-wala`'s matching engine normalizes all invoice numbers by:
1. Stripping all spaces, hyphens, slashes, and special characters.
2. Converting all characters to uppercase.
3. Stripping leading zeroes from the beginning of strings or after alphabetic prefixes.
4. If exact match fails, falling back to a trailing-numeric-substring match.
