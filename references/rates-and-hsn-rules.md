# GST Rates, HSN Codes & Unit Quantity Codes (UQC) Reference

This reference outlines the statutory tax rates, HSN digit rules, and Unit Quantity Codes recognized by the GST portal.

---

## 1. Statutory GST Rate Slabs

| Rate (%) | Applicable Supply Nature | Typical Goods / Services Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **0.0%** | Nil-rated & Exempted | Unbranded food grains, fresh milk, salt, healthcare, schooling |
| **0.1%** | Merchant Exports | Supplies to merchant exporters under Notification 40/2017 |
| **0.25%** | Precious Stones | Cut and polished diamonds, precious and semi-precious stones |
| **1.5%** | Affordable Housing | Construction of affordable residential apartments (without ITC) |
| **3.0%** | Precious Metals | Gold, silver, platinum jewelry |
| **5.0%** | Essential Commodities | Edible oil, sugar, tea, domestic LPG, standard transport services |
| **12.0%** | Standard Low Slabs | Processed food, apparel (> ₹1,000), certain IT hardware |
| **18.0%** | Standard Regular Slabs | Professional services, software, electronics, capital goods, manufacturing |
| **28.0%** | Luxury & Sin Goods | Automobiles, air conditioners, tobacco, aerated drinks (+ Compensation Cess) |

---

## 2. HSN / SAC Digit Rules

- **HSN (Harmonized System of Nomenclature):** For goods (Chapter 01 to Chapter 98).
- **SAC (Services Accounting Code):** For services (Chapter 99).

### Statutory Rules for Table 12 Reporting:
1. **Aggregate Annual Turnover (AATO) up to ₹5 Crore:**
   - Minimum **4 digits** of HSN/SAC are mandatory for B2B supplies.
   - Optional (or 4 digits) for B2C supplies.
2. **Aggregate Annual Turnover (AATO) above ₹5 Crore:**
   - Minimum **6 digits** of HSN/SAC are mandatory for both B2B and B2C supplies.
3. **Exports & Specific Chemical Items:**
   - Mandatory **8 digits**.
4. **Mandatory Separation (May 2025+ Advisory):**
   - HSN summary must be reported separately for **Table 12A (B2B)** and **Table 12B (B2C)**.
