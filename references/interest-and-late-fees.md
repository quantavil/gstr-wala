# Statutory Interest (Section 50) & Late Fees (Section 47) Guide

This reference details the statutory calculations, rates, caps, and legal precedents for interest and late fees under Indian GST laws.

---

## 1. Statutory Interest under Section 50

### Section 50(1) — Delayed Payment of Tax
- **Rate:** **18% per annum** (calculated on a daily basis: $\text{Daily Rate} = \frac{18\%}{365}$).
- **The Net Tax Liability Rule (Proviso to Section 50(1)):**
  - Following the Finance Act 2021 (with retrospective effect from July 1, 2017), interest on delayed filing of returns is payable **strictly on the Net Tax Liability paid through the Electronic Cash Ledger**.
  - **Zero interest** is payable on the portion of tax liability that was offset against Input Tax Credit (ITC) available in the Electronic Credit Ledger!
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Interest Amount} = \text{Net Cash Tax Liability} \times \left(\frac{0.18}{365}\right) \times \text{Delay in Days}$$

### Section 50(3) — Wrongly Availed and Utilized ITC
- **Rate:** **24% per annum**.
- **Crucial Condition:** Interest under Section 50(3) is applicable only when ITC is **both wrongly availed AND utilized** to pay outward tax liability.
- If wrongly availed ITC merely sat idle in the Electronic Credit Ledger without being utilized, no interest is payable upon reversal (retrospective amendment from July 1, 2017).

## 2. Statutory Late Fees under Section 47

Late fee is levied automatically by the GST portal for each day of delay beyond the statutory due date (20th / 22nd / 24th of the succeeding month for GSTR-3B; 11th / 13th for GSTR-1).

### Daily Late Fee Rates
- **Regular (Taxable) Return:** ₹50 per day (₹25 CGST + ₹25 SGST / UTGST).
- **Nil Return:** ₹20 per day (₹10 CGST + ₹10 SGST / UTGST).

### Statutory Maximum Caps on Late Fees (per return)
Under Notification No. 19/2021-CT and subsequent rationalization notifications:

| Return Category / Turnover Slab | Daily Late Fee (CGST + SGST) | Maximum Cap (CGST + SGST) |
|---|---|---|
| **Nil Return (No outward & No inward tax)** | ₹20 / day (₹10 + ₹10) | **₹500** (₹250 CGST + ₹250 SGST) |
| **AATO up to ₹1.5 Crore** | ₹50 / day (₹25 + ₹25) | **₹2,000** (₹1,000 CGST + ₹1,000 SGST) |
| **AATO ₹1.5 Crore to ₹5 Crore** | ₹50 / day (₹25 + ₹25) | **₹5,000** (₹2,500 CGST + ₹2,500 SGST) |
| **AATO above ₹5 Crore** | ₹50 / day (₹25 + ₹25) | **₹10,000** (₹5,000 CGST + ₹5,000 SGST) |

*Note: Late fee for GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B are levied separately. However, GSTR-1 late fee is currently auto-populated into the subsequent month's GSTR-3B Table 5.1.*
