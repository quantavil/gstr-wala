# Departmental Mismatch Notices: DRC-01B (Rule 88C) & DRC-01C (Rule 88D)

This reference explains the automated departmental risk surveillance system, trigger thresholds, legal implications, and required responses for **DRC-01B** and **DRC-01C** intimations.

---

## 1. Form GST DRC-01B (Rule 88C) — Outward Liability Mismatch

### What Triggers DRC-01B?
- Automatically generated by the GST portal when the outward tax liability declared in **Form GSTR-1 / IFF** exceeds the tax declared/paid in **Form GSTR-3B** for the same tax period.
- **Trigger Threshold:** When the difference exceeds **20% AND ₹25,00,000** (or as notified from time to time).

### Mandatory Compliance Timeline (7 Days)
Upon receiving a DRC-01B Part A intimation, the taxpayer must take action within **7 calendar days**:
- **Option 1 (Pay Differential):** Pay the differential tax along with interest under Section 50 using **Form GST DRC-03** and mention the ARN of DRC-03 in **Part B of DRC-01B**.
- **Option 2 (Explain Discrepancy):** Submit a detailed factual explanation in **Part B of DRC-01B** on the portal selecting reasons such as:
  1. Excess liability declared in GSTR-1 due to typographical error.
  2. Advance tax paid in earlier period adjusted in GSTR-3B.
  3. Supplies amended in subsequent period GSTR-1.
  4. Any other valid commercial/accounting reason.

### Legal Consequence of Non-Compliance
- If the taxpayer neither pays nor replies within 7 days, **GSTR-1 / IFF filing for the subsequent tax period is automatically BLOCKED** by the GST portal (Rule 59(6)).

## 2. Form GST DRC-01C (Rule 88D) — Inward ITC Mismatch

### What Triggers DRC-01C?
- Automatically generated by the GST portal when the Input Tax Credit (ITC) availed in **Form GSTR-3B** exceeds the ITC available in **Form GSTR-2B** for that tax period.
- **Trigger Threshold:** When the excess credit claimed exceeds **10% AND ₹1,00,000**.

### Mandatory Compliance Timeline (7 Days)
Upon receiving a DRC-01C Part A intimation, the taxpayer must take action within **7 calendar days**:
- **Option 1 (Reverse Excess ITC):** Reverse the excess credit claimed along with applicable interest under Section 50 using **Form GST DRC-03** and provide the ARN in **Part B**.
- **Option 2 (Justify Difference):** Submit an explanation in **Part B** selecting reasons such as:
  1. ITC reclaimed which was reversed in earlier periods under Table 4(B)(2) / Rule 37.
  2. Import of goods (ICEGATE data not reflected in 2B in time).
  3. Inward RCM supplies where credit is claimed in the month of cash payment.
  4. GSTR-2B generated before supplier's belated filing.

### Legal Consequence of Non-Compliance
- Failure to pay or submit a valid justification within 7 days will automatically block the taxpayer from filing **GSTR-1 / IFF** for the next tax period.
